## Our spirit / 军团的灵魂

For Jessica, JRA is an army with a soul: every bot has a mission and a symbol. Her affection for this team carries a hope—to empower the super-individual, so that no founder has to be alone.

在 Jessica 心中，JRA 是一支具有灵魂的军团：每个 Bot 都有自己的使命与象征。她对这支军团的爱，承载着一个希望——赋能超级个体，让所有创业者不必孤单。

**Motto / 标语：「用智慧链接宇宙，人机共生」**  
Connect the universe through wisdom; humans and machines in symbiosis.

These are role missions and symbolic identities, not claims of completed work, autonomous powers or proven outcomes. Only documented symbols are included; an avatar's appearance alone does not establish its meaning.

这里记录的是岗位使命与象征设定，不代表已完成的工作、自主权限或已验证的成果。只收录有文字依据的象征，不凭头像外观推断寓意。
