# Capital matching and outreach iteration

Reusable workflow only. Operational investor lists, pitch material and private correspondence belong in the owner-private workspace.

1. Match investor stage, sector, geography, cheque size and solo-founder policy against evidence-backed project facts. Link official sources with last-read timestamps. Unknown is not eligible. A shortlist is not an outreach log.
2. Rank candidates by fit and verified deadlines. Generate an internal dated plan and a fallback. Distinguish the official deadline (including timezone) from an internal target.
3. Use the project’s configured business mailbox consistently for registration, login, application, replies and receipts. Preserve historical actual sender headers. Missing mailbox access blocks mailbox-dependent steps; never silently substitute a personal identity.
4. Generate investor-specific pitch, poster, video script and video preview. Change the fit argument as well as the institution name. Preserve originals and hashes. A retitled video with unchanged audio is a draft until old institution references, facts and duration have been checked. Names in artwork imply neither affiliation nor endorsement.
5. Verify exact materials, live demo and submission route. Obtain human approval for each formal submission, supplemental material or outbound message; company, equity and residency commitments remain human decisions. Creating/downloading a package is not sending.
6. After actual dispatch, read and retain the original sent evidence, actual sender/recipient, timestamp/timezone, immutable attachment versions/hashes, receipt and approval. User-entered evidence stays unverified until read. Add accountable next actions and next-check dates to every event.
7. Every verified dispatch starts a strategy review. Record what was learned and save the next version before approaching another investor. Distinguish self-review from actual investor feedback; no response is not rejection. Renaming the recipient alone is not a strategy upgrade.
8. Deduplicate follow-up, track formal feedback separately from auto-receipts, and notify only through validated channels. Continue matching until the founder stops or an explicitly agreed goal is met; never guarantee investment.

## State contract

Candidate → eligibility checked → personalised draft → human approved → dispatched with evidence → receipt checked → follow-up → review and next draft version. Keep blocked, unverified and decision branches explicit. Never promote state from a download, click, generated draft or inaccessible channel.

## Reference integrations

Store candidates and immutable pitch revisions separately from confirmed sent cases. Use optimistic revisions for private record writes and read back after saving. Public examples must use synthetic institutions, placeholder addresses and no founder media. The reference does not connect email/SMS, grant approval, submit applications or change infrastructure.
